howard wen

INGREDIENTS

Duck breast, or leg if you're cheap like me (280g)

Salt, enough to coat (~30g)

2-3g sugar

Cheese cloth, or coffee filter paper

Twine, or rubber bands

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Mix salt and sugar together. Then coat duck leg completely
  1. Put it in a plastic bag and get out as much air as possible
    1. Can submerge open bag in water, making sure to push out all the air without getting water into the bag.
  1. Cure for 24-48 hours.
  1. Rinse all salt off of duck after. Pat it completely dry. Then wrap it in cheese cloth and tie it with the twine.
  1. Hang in fridge for roughly 2 weeks. Gets firm, but make sure it doesn't come out rock hard.
  1. Take it out after and slice into thin strips.
