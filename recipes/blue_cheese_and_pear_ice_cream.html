howard wen

INGREDIENTS

Poached pear blend

3 pears, sliced thin

250mL of white wine (dry preferred)

Some cinnamon

Some sugar

1 tbsp of lemon juice

Ice cream base

2 cups of heavy cream

1 cup of 3% milk

6 large egg yolks

2/3 cup of sugar

1/6 tsp of salt

1 tsp of vanilla

Blue cheese, crumbled as much as possible

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Simmer all ice cream base materials except blue cheese and egg yolks until dissolved and hot
  1. Slowly introduce spoonfuls of cream base into egg yolks, whisking vigorously
    1. Add about a third of the total cream base into the egg yolk mixture
  1. Add egg yolk mixture back into main cream base pot and gently cook until thick
    1. Enough to coat back of spoon
  1. While reducing ice cream base mixture, slowly add in crumbles of blue cheese until all dissolved
    1. Taste and adjust accordingly
  1. Once thick, remove from heat and stir while cooling, then cover with saran wrap so no air contacts the mixture and chill in fridge for 4+ hours
    1. Prevents weird oxidized skin layer on the base
  1. Put all poached pear blend ingredients into pot, boil, then simmer until pears are soft
    1. Watery is ok!
  1. Blend until apple sauce-like consistency, then let cool, then put in fridge to chill for 4+ hours
  1. Churn ice cream base in machine, slowly adding pear blend until fully combined
